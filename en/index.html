Good to Know.

Kavala is the day club inside Hacienda Coyotes: adults-only pool, live DJs, craft cocktails and open-air dining. A relaxed, exclusive atmosphere 40 minutes from Guadalajara in Zapotlanejo, Jalisco.
Fridays: 2:00 PM – 8:00 PM · Saturdays: 3:00 PM – 11:00 PM · Sundays: 3:00 PM – 11:00 PM. Hours may vary for special events — check our Instagram before coming.
Admission to Kavala is free. We do not charge a cover on a regular basis. Some tables and special areas may require a minimum spend. Special events may have a fee, which is announced in advance on our official channels.
